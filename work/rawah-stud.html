Selected project — 2025

Rawah Stud

Logo Design

I build the brand around the idea of equine excellence expressed through the language of classic automotive luxury — not as a literal horse + car logo, but as a sophisticated visual world combining heritage, speed, craftsmanship and prestige.

Where Horsepower Meets Horse Heritage.

Rawah Stud is positioned as a premium horse stable / equestrian establishment where the character of the horse is expressed through the same qualities associated with iconic classic cars: power, elegance, precision, rarity and timeless design.

The brand should feel less like a conventional equestrian business and more like a luxury automotive marque translated into the world of horses.
