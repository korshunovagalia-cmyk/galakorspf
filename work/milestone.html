Selected project — 2024

Milestone

Logo & Brand Identity

Every business has milestones. I make them happen.

Milestone is a marketing agency that helps businesses move from where they are now to where they want to be through strategy, creativity and measurable marketing.

The boy with a paper plane gives the agency a human idea: a big ambition starts with a simple dream.

The boy represents ambition. He is the person who looks beyond the present and imagines something bigger.

The paper plane represents the dream. It is a simple idea with the potential to travel far — just like a business idea, brand or campaign.

Flying high represents growth. The journey from a small beginning to a bigger destination is the essence of a milestone.

Dream small. Think big. Fly high.
