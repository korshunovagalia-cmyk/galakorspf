Noura

Packaging Design

Noura is a private-label food brand created to bring quality, trust, and everyday value to the grocery basket.

The name Noura feels warm, familiar and regional, while its meaning is associated with light and brightness. This creates an opportunity to position the brand around the idea of bringing something good into everyday life.
